# audio_bank — sample credits & licenses

Real foley one-shots bundled with SnapFoley. All are free-license recordings
converted to 16-bit PCM WAV (44.1kHz mono, peak-normalized, trailing silence
trimmed) and split into individual hits.

## Mixkit samples (punches/, basketball/, footsteps/pavement/, footsteps/leaves/, leafcrunch/)

Source: https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/ — preview assets by Mixkit
(Envato). **Mixkit License: free to use in your commercial and non-commercial
projects, no attribution required** (https://mixkit.co/license/).

Punches come from the punch/boxing categories (sfx ids 2155, 2143, 2047,
2153, 2151, 2198), basketball dribbles from the basketball category (2093,
2081, 2090, 2091, 2077, 2076), pavement steps from "Footsteps on heels on the
pavement" (1232), leafy steps from "Walking through dry forest leaves" (533)
and 2428, and the leaf-crunch layer from 751.

## Kenney assets (footsteps/carpet|concrete|grass|snow|wood/)

Source: https://kenney.nl/assets (CC0 1.0 — public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

## Offline guarantee

The bank ships inside the repo; the renderer reads only these local files at
runtime. No network calls, no API keys.
